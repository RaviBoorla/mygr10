Privacy Policy — Rise

Last updated: September 2026

Rise ("the app") is a self-study practice tool for Grade 10 CBSE, ICSE and IB board-exam students, operated by Strat101. This policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Summary

Rise works fully without an account. When you choose to sign in, some data is stored in the cloud so your progress is available across devices. Multiplayer features (Challenge codes and Live Rooms) store only the minimum data needed for those features to work. We do not sell your data and we do not show you ads.

2. Data we collect and why

2a. Without an account (guest use)

Your progress, test attempts, bookmarks, streaks, Short Answer drafts, and settings are stored only in your browser's local storage, on your device. None of this is sent to our servers. Clearing site data or uninstalling the app permanently deletes it.

2b. With an account (optional sign-in)

You may sign in with Google or an email/password via Firebase Authentication (Google LLC). When you do:

2c. Challenge codes (async multiplayer)

When you finish a Free Run and generate a challenge code, the following is stored in Firestore under a random 6-letter code for up to 7 days:

  • Your screen name (or "Anonymous" if not logged in)
  • The list of question IDs served in your run
  • Your score, stage reached, and combo
  • Scores submitted by friends who play the same code

Challenge documents are publicly readable by anyone who knows the code, and expire automatically after 7 days.

2d. Live Rooms (real-time multiplayer)

When you create or join a Live Room, the following is stored in Firestore under a random 6-letter room code for up to 4 hours:

  • Your screen name (or "Guest" if not logged in)
  • Your answers to each question (index only, not the text)
  • Your score
  • The room's question list and game state (current question, timer)

Room documents are publicly readable by anyone who knows the code. They are not cleaned up automatically but contain no identifying information beyond the name you choose to display.

4. No advertising or behavioural tracking

Rise does not embed advertising SDKs, analytics/tracking scripts, or any mechanism that profiles your behaviour for advertising. We do not share your data with any third party for marketing purposes.

5. Children's privacy

Rise is designed for school students. Guest use collects no personal data. If a child creates an account, the data stored is limited to what is described in section 2b above. Parents or guardians may request deletion of a child's account and associated data by emailing us at rise@strat101.com.

6. Your rights and choices

  • Use without an account: All core study features work without signing in.
  • Edit your profile: Screen name, country, and city can be updated at any time from the profile modal (avatar → profile).
  • Delete your account: Email rise@strat101.com to request deletion of your account and all associated Firestore data.
  • Local data: Clear your browser's site data for rise.strat101.com to erase local storage at any time.

7. Data retention

Account and sync data is retained until you request deletion. Challenge codes are stored for 7 days. Live Room data persists until manually cleaned up (typically within days). Local storage data remains on your device until you clear it.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change. We encourage you to review it periodically.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to rise@strat101.com.
